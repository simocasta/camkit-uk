Intended use

CamKIT is a research prototype of a clinical decision-support aid, published for research use only. It is an aid to, not a substitute for, clinical judgement — it does not replace clinical examination or imaging. It is intended for evaluation by healthcare professionals, in the context of patients aged 16 and over presenting with acute knee injury after fracture has been excluded.

Developed and validated on clinician-completed data. Not validated for patient self-assessment. Not a registered medical device — confirm the regulatory position with your institution before any clinical-pathway use.

Evidence

On retrospective clinician-completed data, the three-tier CamKIT showed sensitivity 100%, specificity 94.3%, PPV 89% and NPV 100% for clinically significant injury. In prospective patient-completed use, the rule-out (Low) signal was preserved but overall discrimination and rule-in precision were lower — interpret patient-entered scores with caution.

  • Molloy T, Gompels B, Castagno S, McCaskie A, McDonnell S. The Cambridge Knee Injury Tool (CamKIT): a clinical prediction tool for acute soft tissue knee injuries. BMJ Open Sport Exerc Med 2025;11(1):e002357. doi:10.1136/bmjsem-2024-002357
  • Molloy T, Gompels B, McDonnell S. Assessing diagnostic challenges in acute soft-tissue knee injuries: a Delphi study. Bone Jt Open 2024;5(11):984–91. doi:10.1302/2633-1462.511.BJO-2024-0159.R1
  • Molloy T, et al. Feasibility study of the Cambridge knee injury tool (CamKIT): 18-month analysis of acute knee injuries at an urgent treatment centre. J Clin Orthop Trauma 2025;70:103203. doi:10.1016/j.jcot.2025.103203
